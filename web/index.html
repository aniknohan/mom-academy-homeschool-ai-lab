How this plan works

Each school day has 7 short parts and takes about 45 to 60 minutes. You don't have to do it all at once. The Printables tab has the pages for each day: cards, mats, tracing pages and charts. Most use only paper, crayons, scissors and glue.

This year follows the same 19 themes and letters as Playing Preschool Year 1. Next year is Playing Preschool Year 2 (still preschool). Kindergarten comes the year after.

No books to buy. Every reading step has a free option: your library, the Khan Academy Kids Library, Unite for Literacy or Storyline Online.

  • No rush, no pressure. Nothing needs to be mastered this year.
  • Letter names first. Say the sound, but knowing each letter's shape and name is the main goal.
  • Alphabet book. Keep every Friday letter page in a folder.
  • Apps last. Hands-on lesson first, then 10 to 15 minutes of Khan Academy Kids or ABCmouse.

Supplies for the year

Paper, crayons, pencils, child scissors, glue stick, tape, a few clear sheet protectors, playdough, something to count (beans, pasta, buttons), a die, a deck of cards, a folder for the alphabet book, and a free library card. Each theme's extra supplies are listed on its first Monday.

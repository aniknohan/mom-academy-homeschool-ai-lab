# Teaching Guide

Every day includes a one-page guide for the grown-up. It's shown in the Lesson view under **Teaching guide** and printed as the first sheet of the day.

## What each guide contains

| Section | What it gives the parent |
|---|---|
| Today's goals | The letter, math and theme goal in one line each |
| Morning circle | The exact words for the calendar routine |
| Letters & sounds | **Say**, **Ask**, **Easier**, **Harder** and **Watch for**, matched to the day's letter activity (meet, hunt, review, alphabet page or listening game) |
| Math | Three tips chosen by skill: counting, sorting, comparing, shapes, patterns or putting together |
| Theme | The **think-and-talk question** of the day and a **word of the day** with a kid-friendly meaning |
| Read-aloud | The comprehension skill for that weekday: predicting, remembering details, prior knowledge, making connections or retelling |
| Fine motor | A grip, scissors or writing tip for the current phase |
| Praise to try | A growth-mindset phrase ("You kept trying, even when it was hard!") |

## Think-and-talk questions

There are 190 questions, one per day, written for each theme. Examples:

- *Apples:* "Why do you think an apple floats but a rock sinks?"
- *Construction:* "Why are triangles so strong?"
- *Fairy tales:* "Why did the slow tortoise win the race?"

The guide reminds the parent to wait 5 seconds, accept every answer and ask "Why do you think that?". That habit builds reasoning more than any worksheet.

## Words of the week

There are 152 theme words (8 per theme, 4 each week), each with a short child-friendly meaning. One is highlighted as the word of the day, to use three times.

# Printables

Mom Academy builds **1,145 printable pages**: 1,140 for the 190 days plus a 5-page circle-time kit. Every sheet is US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and prints in black and white. Most need only paper, crayons, scissors and glue.

![Sample printables](screenshots/printables-sample.png)

## By weekday

| Day | Pages |
|---|---|
| Every day | **Teaching guide**, plus one **fine-motor** page (it changes through the year's 4 phases) |
| Monday | **Sorting page** with cut-out picture cards, **words of the week**, **what comes next?** patterns (AB → ABB/AAB → ABC), plus **theme word cards** and the **song card** in a theme's first week, or **"What I know about…"** in its second week |
| Tuesday | **Letter card**, **letter hunt cards**, **find the letter** grid, **number cards** for the week's numbers |
| Wednesday | **Compare page** (size, length, weight, full/empty, position, loud/quiet, textures, odd-one-out or measuring), **count and graph**, **my activity page** (I think / what happened) |
| Thursday | **Build the letter** (playdough mat), **starts-with picture sort**, **letter maze**, **five or ten frame(s)**, plus **picture counters** in a theme's first week |
| Friday | **Alphabet book page** (new letter) or **letter match game** (review), **shape of the week** or **count and circle**, **retell the story**, **weekly check-in** |

## Circle-time kit (print once)

A "Today is…" board with weather pictures, days of the week, months of the year, an A–Z alphabet chart and a 0–20 number line.

## Printing tips

- In the app, open **Printables**, then choose **Print today's pages**, **Print the whole week** or **Print this page**.
- Choose **Letter** paper, or A4 with "Fit to page". Choose **Save as PDF** to keep a copy.
- Pages used all year (mats, frames, number cards) last longer in clear sheet protectors.

# Privacy

Mom Academy has **no server, no account, no ads and no analytics**. Nothing is sent over the internet.

| What | Where it is stored |
|---|---|
| Days done, ticked parts, checklist | On the device (`localStorage`) |
| Child's first name (only used on name-tracing pages) | On the device |
| Start date, theme choice | On the device |
| Windows app copy | `%APPDATA%\MomAcademy\progress.json` (+ `.bak`) |

- **Backup:** go to **Setup → Save backup file**. On Android this opens the share sheet, so you can save to Drive, Files or email.
- **Restore:** go to **Setup → Restore from backup** and choose the `.json` file.
- **Remove everything:** **Setup → Reset progress**, or uninstall the app.

The only links that leave the app are to free reading sites (Khan Academy Kids, Unite for Literacy, Storyline Online). They open in your browser.

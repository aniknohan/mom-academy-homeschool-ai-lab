# Curriculum

Mom Academy is a **gentle first preschool year**: 38 weeks, 190 days, 19 two-week themes. It focuses on play, talk, letter recognition and early number sense, and nothing needs to be "mastered". A second year (letter sounds and blending) is on the roadmap.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Letters & sounds | Math | Also |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Listening game (rhymes, syllables) | Sorting and predicting | Theme song, words of the week, patterns |
| Tuesday | Meet or review the letter | Numbers and counting | Letter hunt, find-the-letter page |
| Wednesday | Listening game | Measuring and comparing | Picture graph, activity page |
| Thursday | Letter hunt and playdough letter | Five and ten frames | Letter maze, starts-with picture sort |
| Friday | Alphabet book page or letter review | Shapes or review | Retell, weekly check-in |

Each day has 7 parts (about 45–60 minutes): **morning circle, letters & sounds, math, theme, read-aloud, fine motor, app time**.

## Progressions

- **Letters:** one letter per theme (two in a few themes), in a non-alphabetical order so each letter is learned on its own. Fridays alternate between building an alphabet book page and reviewing every letter so far.
- **Math:** counting to 3 → 5 → 10 → 20, one-to-one counting, more and less, number bonds through play, 2D and 3D shapes, comparing size, length and weight, and simple "putting together".
- **Fine motor:** four phases over the year:
  1. Playdough, tearing, tongs and tracing lines and circles
  2. Snipping, cutting straight lines and tracing the child's name
  3. Cutting curves, copying shapes and letters, and lacing
  4. Cutting out shapes, drawing a person and writing the child's name independently
- **Thinking:** a daily open question, predictions before every experiment, and retelling with first, next and last.

## End-of-year goals

Recognizes most capital letters and some lowercase letters, hears rhymes, claps syllables, counts to 20, counts 10 objects accurately, knows numbers 0–10, sorts and makes AB patterns, names four basic shapes, cuts on a line, tries to write their name, follows 2-step directions and retells a simple story. The in-app **Checklist** tracks all of these.
